# Instrument sample credits

The violin, cello and contrabass samples in this folder come from
[tonejs-instruments](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments) by
Nicholaus Brosowsky, licensed under
[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). They were gathered
from Freesound and public-domain sources (the cello pack is by
[flcellogrl](https://freesound.org/people/flcellogrl/packs/12408/)).

There are no viola samples, so the viola part is played with cello samples
(C3-B4) and violin samples (C5 and up). The "string ensemble 1" track
(1st & 2nd violins) reuses the violin samples.
